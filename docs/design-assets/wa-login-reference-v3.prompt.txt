Input image 1 shows the current login. Its problem: only the heading looks like a scroll while the account/password boxes look like a generic modern form. REBUILD THE ENTIRE LOGIN AS ONE COHERENT LIGHTWEIGHT JAPANESE PAPER OBJECT. Input image 2 establishes the desired woodblock, sumi ink and vermilion design language. Use its look, not its mistaken incidental text or feature names.
Create a detailed high-resolution design sheet. Left two-thirds: one large elegant smartphone login screen in full, with the form clearly readable. Right one-third: magnified specimens of the account input in focus, password input with a small functional visibility button, the primary login button in enabled and disabled states, and a clear inline error. These are a design specification, not additional controls on the live screen.
Login screen content only: small identity stamp 講 and role 旅行者 at top. ONE unfurled vertical paper scroll holds the whole screen content, with understated wood spindles at top and bottom, subtle folds and thin paper edge, clean ivory writing surface. Heading ログイン printed directly on this same sheet, NOT a separate horizontal scroll plaque. A modest original woodblock illustration band of travelers/pines/birds above the form, no photographic card. Below it Japanese labels アカウント名 and パスワード in legible dark ink. Each input is a spacious writing area integrated in the paper, ruled with a fine slightly irregular ink baseline and short corner marks; NO generic white rounded rectangle floating inside the paper. Keep a solid quiet surface under typed text. Example typed account aoi and password bullets. Password visibility control 表示 is a small ink-framed stamp-like action with a large comfortable touch area. Main button ログイン → is a solid deep vermilion rectangular seal/tab with thin double irregular engraved border, subtle pale inner line, clear white letters, min48px high. Full width aligned with writing lines. A small unobtrusive ink mark terminates the bottom of the form; no slogans, no footers, no account list, no social login.
The same visual grammar must cover focus (dark ink underline + small vermilion indicator), error (clear Japanese エラー message under field and vermilion rule), disabled (muted pigment with readable text), input caret, labels, visibility button, button hover. Avoid texture behind text. Ordinary readable Gothic for labels/controls, Mincho only heading. Not a children's cartoon, not grungy brown antique paper, not a glossy modern card, not gold ornate fantasy UI. Colors ivory #f8f5ed, sumi #292d28, vermilion #9b3d2c, subdued moss. Create the tactile subtle artistry through edges, printing, proportions and spacing. Balanced, lightweight, inviting. Realistic touch areas, 430px phone design. No fake scrollbars, no duplicated heading, no generic decorative marketing copy.

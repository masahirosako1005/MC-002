Design direction: Japanese makimono scrolls, bound travel journals, vermilion hand-carved hanko stamps, black mokuhanga artwork and crow-and-path motifs inspired by the graphic balance of Kumano Goofu. This is a welcoming contemporary usable app built from traditional illustrated paper objects, light and sophisticated, not a sepia antique shop, not a futuristic dashboard, not rounded SaaS cards with a Japanese font pasted on.

Main top 80% shows a beautifully composed 1440x900-ish desktop stage with two large readable phone screens on a very pale ivory background, generous clear outer whitespace.
